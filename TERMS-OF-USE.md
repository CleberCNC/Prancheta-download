## 4. Your responsibility when machining

A CNC machine can cause serious accidents and damage. By using the program you declare that you understand this and that you are fully responsible for using the machine. In particular, it is up to you to:

- Check the G-code and the simulation before each job: toolpaths, depths, heights and cutting direction.
- Check the sizes and the zero of the material, the part and the machine, as well as how the part is clamped.
- Enter the right tool data: diameter, cutting length, feed rate and spindle speed.
- Choose and check the right post-processor for your machine. Post-processors you edit are your responsibility.
- Follow safety rules: wear protective equipment, never leave the machine running unattended and know how to stop it in an emergency.
- Run a test (in the air, on cheap material or at reduced speed) whenever you use a toolpath, a tool or a machine for the first time.

The simulation warnings (rapid move inside the material, cut deeper than the bit and others) are a help, not a guarantee. The program does not find every problem. Machining times, the setup sheet and calculated sizes are estimates.
